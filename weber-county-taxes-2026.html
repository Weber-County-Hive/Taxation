Weber County in 2026

The county's new sales tax

Weber County did not raise its property tax rate for 2025 or 2026. Instead, the three commissioners voted unanimously in June 2026 to impose the 0.2% county transportation sales tax ("5th 5th"), effective Oct. 1, 2026, about $13.1 million a year. The combined sales tax rate in most Weber cities goes from 7.25% to 7.45%. The power was first granted in 2018 (S.B. 136) and rewritten by S.B. 260 (2023) and S.B. 242 (2026). State law allowed, but did not require, a ballot opinion question. None was held.

Of the $13.1 million: $3.27 million to the 16 cities and unincorporated county for transportation; $3.27 million to the county for transportation or public safety; and $6.54 million to the county for any purpose the commission chooses through Sept. 30, 2029, then half transportation and half FrontRunner projects (WFRC, as reported by KSL Sept. 17, 2026).

At the register: 20 cents on $100, $2 on $1,000. Full case file: A Public Vote, but No Vote by the Public.

Other local choices in Weber County

  • West Haven charges no city property tax. It will collect a 6% energy tax on electricity and natural gas starting Oct. 1, 2026. That city power comes from long-standing Utah law, not from the bills above.
  • Hotel tax: Weber County is one of 20 counties that raised the county transient room tax by up to 0.25% under H.B. 456, effective Oct. 1, 2025 (Tax Commission Bulletin 13-25).
  • Roy voters decide Prop 10 on Nov. 3, 2026: keep the $1,749,050 increase or go back to the certified rate.
  • 2025: the State Tax Commission denied increases from Roy, North Ogden, South Ogden, Harrisville, North View Fire and Central Weber Sewer, citing tightened notice rules from S.B. 29 (2024) and S.B. 202 (2025).
  • A low estimate of what's already on the books: six rate increases since 2022 by Weber County (including the sheriff), Weber Fire, Weber Basin Water, Central Weber Sewer and Weber School District add $255.42 a year on a $434,583 median home. It leaves out school bonds, city taxes, the new sales tax and new PIDs (Hive tracker, Sept. 2026).

Water conservancy districts are the only type here whose board members are appointed rather than elected by voters. The two on the list are Weber Basin Water ($1,738,797 across five counties) and Central Utah Water ($1,716,903 across seven).

What the records do not show

No record we found says a city, county or district raised a tax because the state cut income taxes. Income tax is a state tax; cutting it doesn't take money out of local budgets directly.
The property exemptions above do move costs to other local taxpayers, by the fiscal notes' own estimates. Those amounts are small per home.
How much growth sent to zones costs each county, school district or fire district has not been measured in the records reviewed.
